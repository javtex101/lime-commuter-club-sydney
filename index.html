What is the Lime Commuter Club?

The Lime Commuter Club gives Sydney's team a direct channel to its most active riders, real intelligence for operational decisions, and a pipeline of community voices for government relations.

Currently

No Communications

There is no existing mechanism to reach Sydney's most engaged riders directly.

Solution

Contact Database

Owned, actionable segments that the brand can reach independently of third-parties.

Currently

No Reporting Channel

Riders have no structured way to share service feedback or advocacy testimonials.

Solution

Community Intelligence Tool

Member feedback on locations and experiences feeds directly into operations and council submissions.

Currently

No Proactive Brand Voice

Safety messaging, parking guidance and responsible riding are rarely communicated proactively.

Solution

Positive Behaviour Push

First Rider Academies, parking guidance and referral incentives strengthen Lime's opinion in the public eye.

Currently

No Retention

Each activation starts from scratch, eroding trust and letting warm leads go cold.

Solution

Retention and Acquisition Flywheel

Events, perks and recognition increase lifetime value and turn loyal members into new rider referrals.

Sydney parking guide for Lime riders

How do I know where to park in Sydney?

Depending on where you end your trip in Sydney, you will either need to park your Lime in a parking bay, bike rack or neatly to the side of the footpath. All this is shown in the app.

🅿 P Bay🚲 Bike Rack🛤 Footpath

How to park in a P bay

In certain areas parking bays are mandatory. These show in the app with a P when you end your ride.

Both wheels inside the markings
Park in line with other vehicles
If bay is full, Lime to a different bay

Areas with Mandatory Parking Zones

BondiCoogeeManlyCBDNewtownPyrmont

How to park in a bike rack

In line/parallel with the rack
Leave space for other cyclists
If full, Lime to another rack

How to park in areas with no P

Close to the edge, kickstand away from road
In line with lampposts, bins, trees
Leave space for wheelchair users and prams

Acing the end-of-trip photo

The photo you take at the end of your trip is really important. We review photos to see how you parked.

Wide shot, show the whole bike
Show context, include the surrounding area
Check the rules, confirm correct parking first

What happens if you do not park correctly?

Warning first. Repeated offences result in a charge fee up to $25. Repeat offenders may be banned.

Case Study
Balmain Bike Bus
In April we partnered with Balmain Rozelle Climate Change to support their Balmain Bike Bus, a weekly guided ride into the city to introduce commuters to available safe cycling infrastructure.
Run by Rebecca Fernandez (Inner West Active Transport Committee Member) and Tim Hand (Bike Leichhardt Member), this co-hosted event served as a way to get involved with local advocacy, answer questions about Lime’s services and build better relations with local stakeholders.

Bringing it all together

Strategic Framework

The Lime Commuter Club flywheel merges different customer touchpoints together.

Satisfied customers drive new customer acquisition through word of mouth and referrals. Every stage reinforces the others, creating compounding growth.

The more we increase efficiency and remove friction while aligning goals with community, the more powerful this flywheel becomes.
