Sydney parking guide for Lime riders

How do I know where to park in Sydney?

Depending on where you end your trip in Sydney, you will either need to park your Lime in a parking bay, bike rack or neatly to the side of the footpath. All this is shown in the app.

🅿 P Bay🚲 Bike Rack🛤 Footpath

How to park in a P bay

In certain areas parking bays are mandatory. These show in the app with a P when you end your ride.

Both wheels inside the markings
Park in line with other vehicles
If bay is full, Lime to a different bay

Areas with Mandatory Parking Zones

BondiCoogeeManlyCBDNewtownPyrmont

How to park in a bike rack

In line/parallel with the rack
Leave space for other cyclists
If full, Lime to another rack

How to park in areas with no P

Close to the edge, kickstand away from road
In line with lampposts, bins, trees
Leave space for wheelchair users and prams

Acing the end-of-trip photo

The photo you take at the end of your trip is really important. We review photos to see how you parked.

Wide shot, show the whole bike
Show context, include the surrounding area
Check the rules, confirm correct parking first

What happens if you do not park correctly?

Warning first. Repeated offences result in a charge fee up to $25. Repeat offenders may be banned.

Next Steps

The Lime Commuter Club aims to close the gap between what Lime has been achieving with the Sydneysiders that use it. Starting as a mailing list for communications awareness, we have broad visions for expansion, including more in-person events to drive acquisition and engagement and partnerships to drive loyalty.
